# Packaging & Pricing Guide

How to bundle LeadFlowHQ's features into services you sell to local small
businesses. These are starting-point numbers based on common local-service
marketing agency pricing — adjust for your market and costs.

## The pitch, in one line

"We make sure you never lose a customer to a missed call, a slow reply, or
an outdated website — and we make it easy to get the reviews you deserve."

## Suggested tiers

### Starter — Lead Capture — $297/mo (+ $497 one-time setup)
- Missed-call text-back on a dedicated tracking number
- Instant lead follow-up from their website contact form
- New-lead SMS alerts to the owner
- Shared dashboard login to see every lead and message
- Best for: businesses that just want to stop losing calls/leads

### Growth — Lead Capture + Reputation — $497/mo
- Everything in Starter
- Automated review request texts/emails after every completed job
- Monthly performance summary (leads captured, calls saved, reviews sent)
- Best for: businesses actively trying to grow their Google review count

### Pro — Full Local Presence — $897/mo (+ $1,500–$3,000 one-time site build)
- Everything in Growth
- A professional, SEO-optimized website (from one of the included
  templates, customized with their branding/photos)
- Ongoing site hosting and updates
- Priority support / faster response SLA
- Best for: businesses with an outdated or no website

### Common add-ons (à la carte)
- Additional tracking number / location: +$49/mo each
- Extra website page or landing page: +$150–$300 one-time
- Google Business Profile optimization: +$150/mo
- Paid ad management (Google/Meta): +15% of ad spend or flat $500+/mo

## Why this pricing works

Your hard costs per client are small: a Twilio number (~$1.15/mo) plus a
few cents per SMS/call, and SendGrid's free tier covers email at this
volume. Hosting the whole platform costs $5–$25/mo total regardless of how
many clients you add. That means gross margin on the Starter tier alone is
typically 90%+, and the Pro tier's one-time site-build fee mostly covers
your time.

## Sales talking points

Missed calls are money walking out the door — most home service and
local businesses convert 20–40% of answered calls into booked jobs, so a
single recovered call often pays for a month of service. Response speed
matters even more for web leads: research on lead conversion consistently
shows that replying within 5 minutes converts dramatically better than
replying an hour later, and most small businesses take far longer than
that without automation. Reviews compound over time — a steady drip of
fresh 5-star reviews is one of the few levers that keeps moving a
business's local search ranking and click-through rate upward, and it's
something most owners know they should do but never get around to.

## Onboarding checklist for a new client

1. Create the client in the dashboard (Clients → Add a new client).
2. Buy a Twilio number, set the Voice/SMS webhooks to the URLs shown on
   the client's page.
3. Set their forward-to number, Google review link, and (if applicable)
   Yelp review link.
4. Add the lead-capture snippet to their website contact form, or deploy
   one of the included templates for them.
5. Send a test lead and a test call to confirm everything fires correctly.
6. Walk them through the dashboard so they can see leads/messages
   themselves, or agree to send them a weekly recap instead.

## What to say when a prospect asks "why not just do this myself?"

They can — the individual pieces (a Twilio number, a contact form, asking
for reviews) aren't secret. What they're paying for is having it all set
up correctly, monitored, and maintained without spending their own time on
it, plus a single point of accountability if something breaks. That's a
totally fair objection to expect and a fine one to be upfront about.
